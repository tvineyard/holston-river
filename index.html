How long the water takes to reach each point

Hours from generation start to first rise. Anchored on TVA river-forecasting guidance — 6–12 h at River Ranch, 18–30 h near Knoxville — with celerity scaling as Q0.66. The spread column is how much longer the release takes to pass than it took to leave — a 3-hour release at the dam is an 8-hour rise at your place, and a correspondingly smaller one. That is why a one-hour minimum-flow pulse arrives at Nances Ferry as ~810 cfs and floats nobody.
